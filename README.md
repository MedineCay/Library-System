On the main screen of the program, there should be a menu where the user will choose one
of the operations;
1. Create New Customer (C_ID auto increment, name, surname, age, wallet)
2. Deposit Money to the Customer (C_ID, wallet)
3. Add New Book (B_ID auto increment, name, author, age limit, price per week, rented)
4. Rent A Book (R_ID auto increment, C_ID, B_ID, rented date, week)
5. Delivery A Book (R_ID, remaining week / late week)
6. Burn Book (B_ID, rented)
7. Update Customer Information (name, surname, age)
8. Update Book Information (name, author, age limit, price per week)
9. List of Customers Who Rent a Book
10. List of Customers
11. List of Books
12. Search Book (by name, author, or age limit)
Note: In this library, there is only one of each book.
Note: The information in brackets is additional information to help you, do not show them
on the console.
